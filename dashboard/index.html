How the radar works

implied = TVL × 0.30 × (1 + clamp(7d, −60%, +120%) × 0.6) × (0.55 + min(age, 1095d) ÷ 1095 × 0.45) × (1 + min(chains − 1, 4) × 0.025)
  • TVL is the size of the business a token would capitalise. Seven-day growth is its direction. Age with no token is how long the launch has been deferred. Chain breadth is how widely the business is already distributed.
  • Coverage: all 40 watchlist protocols, plus every other rankable pre-token protocol below the $50M floor — 226 rows, not a sample. Versioned pools (a name ending in V2, 2.0 and the like) and exchanges are left out.
  • “Records no token” is not “has no token.” DefiLlama's record is not proof: a protocol on this board may still carry a token its record omits. Nothing here predicts whether or when a token launches, and nothing here is financial advice.
  • Farm plans are derived from each protocol's own category, chains, TVL and age — never from a hand-written list of names. Each step states what it costs and how long it takes. Never pay to claim: a real claim is always free to check.
